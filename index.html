BusyCal for Mac

BusyCal is the powerful, flexible calendar app for macOS. Packed with time-saving features, it syncs seamlessly with iCloud, Google, Exchange, and more. View and manage your schedule in customizable Day, Week, Month, Year, and List views—tailored precisely to your needs.

Customizable Views

Adjust the number of weeks in Month view or days in Week view. Customize fonts, sizes, colors, and time formats for a calendar that fits your workflow perfectly. Scrolling Month and Week views keep everything fluid and accessible.

Integrated Tasks and Weather

Create repeating To Dos that appear in your calendar and carry forward until done. See live weather forecasts, sunrise/sunset times, and moon phases directly in your view. Add graphics or virtual sticky notes for quick reminders.

Seamless Syncing

Sync effortlessly with iCloud, Google Calendar, Exchange, MobileMe, or other BusyCal users via LAN or internet. Share calendars with password protection and SSL encryption. Fully iCal compatible for broad interoperability.

Powerful Event Management

Edit events in a non-modal info panel. Filter and sort customizable List views by date, calendar, or more. New keyboard shortcuts, natural language parsing, and travel time calculations make scheduling effortless.

Refined Details

Version 2024.4.2 brings faster Task List reloads with animations, colored tags, improved multi-line location syncing, and precise countdowns accounting for travel. Events open quicker, with subtle UI cues like the Today button.
